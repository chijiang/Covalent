# Experience and validation reference

## Journey

Describe the person's goal, the trigger, key steps, friction, support, and desired outcome. Only include steps relevant to the present decision. Mark uncertain observations as hypotheses.

## Idea and prototype

Offer two or three meaningfully different approaches when comparison helps. Show a small sample interaction, rough layout, or scenario before investing in a full artifact. State what the sample leaves untested.

## Feedback

Ask what the person could understand or do after seeing the prototype, where they hesitated, and what real-world constraint changed their response. Revise based on observed feedback rather than defending the first idea.
