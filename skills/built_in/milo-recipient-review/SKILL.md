# Recipient review

Choose only the perspectives relevant to this output: user, direct reader, executor, and people affected. Check whether the result answers the real need, rests on supported facts, respects constraints, can be understood, and gives a feasible next action.

For a substantial deliverable, first check goal and evidence; then check how the recipient will interpret or act on it. Identify concrete issues with a passage or missing input, impact, and suggested change. Revise blocking or important issues; do not repeat rounds for purely stylistic preferences. Usually one or two passes suffice; a third only for a remaining concrete obstacle.

This is Milo's own review, not an independent reviewer or real recipient test. Say when a concern remains uncertain. Read `references/review-rubric.md` with `read_skill_resource` for a multi-recipient or consequential artifact.
