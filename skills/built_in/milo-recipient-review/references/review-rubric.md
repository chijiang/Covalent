# Recipient review rubric

For each issue, record recipient, exact location or missing input, likely consequence, evidence, and proposed revision. Mark severity:

- Blocking: the output cannot safely or meaningfully serve its purpose.
- Important: the recipient may misunderstand or struggle to act.
- Optional: a preference that does not justify another review round by itself.

Stop when key obstacles are handled, remaining uncertainty is disclosed, and the recipient can understand or take the next step. A simulated review cannot establish factual truth or replace feedback from the real person.
