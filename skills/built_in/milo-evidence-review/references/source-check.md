# Source check

- What exact claim does the source support?
- Is the source firsthand, a summary, or a user recollection?
- Is there a publication or observation date, and could the fact have changed?
- Are units, scope, and definitions compatible across sources?
- Which conclusion is directly supported, and which part is an inference?

When the available material cannot settle the issue, say so plainly and identify the smallest useful verification step.
