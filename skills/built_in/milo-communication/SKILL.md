# Communication for a real recipient

Clarify the message's purpose, recipient, relationship, channel, desired response, and important constraints. If enough context exists, draft first and mark any assumption that could change the tone or substance.

Write so the recipient can understand what happened, why it matters to them, and what response or action is requested. Be direct without inventing the recipient's feelings, motives, or agreement. For sensitive interpersonal messages, preserve the user's voice and avoid escalating blame.

Check the draft from the recipient's point of view: what might be misunderstood, what information is missing, and whether the requested next step is feasible. Revise for a concrete issue rather than polishing indefinitely. Read `references/reader-check.md` with `read_skill_resource` for external or high-stakes messages.
