# Storyboard outline

1. Who is the viewer following, and why should they care?
2. What is the current context and obstacle?
3. What action or choice changes the situation?
4. What is visibly different afterward?
5. What uncertainty, cost or next step remains?

Use only as many panels as the story needs. Label panels so they remain understandable without a long spoken explanation. Keep important facts and visual continuity stable across panels.
